THE IDEA

What is electric flux?

Electric flux is a measure of the electric field passing through a given surface.

For a uniform electric field passing through a flat surface, the flux depends on the strength of the field, the area of the surface and its orientation relative to the field.

Φ = EA cos θ
01

Electric Field

A stronger electric field produces greater flux through the same surface.

02

Area

Increasing the area of the surface can increase the electric flux.

03

Orientation

The angle between the electric field and the surface's normal affects the flux.
