THE IDEA

What is an electric field?

An electric field is the region around a charged particle in which another electric charge would experience an electric force.

The electric field at a point is defined as the electric force experienced per unit positive test charge placed at that point.

E = F q
01

Direction

The electric field direction is the direction of the force on a positive test charge.

02

Magnitude

For a point charge, the field becomes weaker as the distance from the charge increases.

03

Superposition

Electric fields from multiple charges combine according to the principle of superposition.
